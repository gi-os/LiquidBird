# LiquidBird

Theos tweak injected into the NeoFreeBird IPA (Twitter-branded sideload IPA from the latest NeoFreeBird release: BHTwitter, branding already reverted; v7.3.0 = Twitter 12.38.1, built with the iOS 27.1 SDK). Personal sideload only.

## Goals
1. Real iOS 26 Liquid Glass: floating glass pill/rail for the tab bar, glass on nav bars and post-detail buttons. X ships this itself (XNavigation.TabBarPill, XDesignSystem.Glass, TFNGlass*) behind the `ios_liquid_glass_*` feature switches; NeoFreeBird forces them with its Labs toggle (`liquid_glass` in NSUserDefaults), which LiquidBird defaults on. Hook glass in ourselves only where X's redesign leaves gaps.
2. Remove Grok everywhere: tab, per-tweet button, post-detail floating button, "..." menu items.
3. Remove Live / Spaces / broadcasts from timelines and the Spaces bar.
4. Ads, premium upsells and banners: rely on NeoFreeBird's BHTwitter toggles first; hook only what survives.

## Layout
- `Tweak.x`             Grok/Live removal; defaults for NeoFreeBird's own toggles (liquid_glass, hide_quick_compose, hide_spaces, Grok, ads) via registerDefaults, since Giovanni can't reach its settings; collapses the live sports carousel
- `Tiling.x`            iPhone Duo's wide poses (window >= 600pt): Twitter for iPad's layout after gi-os/twitter's TabStackView/ClassicSidebar, in Liquid Glass. A glass column in the trailing strip the status bar runs down: a rail of the tabs (Grok left out) ending in compose as its last row (XTabbedAppNavigationViewController handleComposeTweetKeyCommand; X's own + fades out with the whole TabBarChromeContainerView, since its glass container draws the + whatever the button's alpha) and a gear opening NeoFreeBird's settings (BHTManager BHTSettingsWithAccount:, whose own Settings row doesn't show on these layouts). The rail mirrors X's glass tab bar (XNavigation TabBarItemView, selected with accessibilityActivate) or the older T1TabBarViewController; the tab bar fades out meanwhile. With room for two columns the current tab is a centred column card. Step 2 is `Columns.x`.
  The Duo folded is told by its status bar's strip down the trailing edge (`LBSideStrip`), remembered per window size: hiding the status bar (the media viewer, the slide-over menu) takes the strip's inset away, so T1HostViewController's additional safe area stands in for it meanwhile and nothing under it relayouts (that had scrolled the timeline).
- `Columns.x`           step 2 of the tiling, where there's a 400pt column plus a 160pt sliver beside the sidebar (the Duo unfolded): pushes on a tab's stack (`XNAVNavigationController pushViewController:animated:`) open as columns in a strip over the tab's root instead (gi-os/twitter's TabStackView): after the column last touched, replacing later ones, newest at the trailing edge, snapping to column edges; each column is an XNAVNavigationController child of X's TabBarController with a close button. Leaving the tab closes them; folding pushes them back onto the stack. Tapping outside the columns (the dimmed tab) or swiping them away (left past the end; right past the start when they're on the right) closes them all. With 600pt beside the sidebar the selected tab narrows (its bars through the safe area's sides; its screen is placed in its side outright, `LBCFitScreen`, since X's timelines and Explore's pages sit in paging cells that drop a safe area's sides) and X's own Explore tab (GuideContainerViewController in its XNAV, which X keeps loaded and hidden behind the selected tab) is shown as the panel on its right, like X desktop's right sidebar; `UILayoutContainerView -setHidden:` keeps it shown while it's the panel. X makes a tab the first time it's shown; until Explore has been opened the panel's place says to open it once (switching tabs on its behalf at launch broke Home's first load). As the panel its bar's buttons (gear, +) go. Wherever there's room the feed is narrow; a two-finger swipe moves it a step left or right (or the sidebar logo's long-press menu picks): left, centre (X's own full-width feed, no Explore panel) or right (`liquidbird_feed_side`, -1/0/1; older builds kept `liquidbird_feed_right`), columns opening over the other side, and the menu turns the Explore panel off (`liquidbird_explore_panel`), leaving black. The layout copy's header says the panel's state. Not yet: pull-down to close, wide web panels, the fold (book pose).
- `Debug.x`             three-finger long press copies the view/VC hierarchy (classes and frames, no text) for Giovanni to paste; how class names get verified without FLEX
- `LiquidBird.plist`    bundle filter (com.atebits.Tweetie2)
- `scripts/patch_glass.sh <in.ipa> <out.ipa>`  strips UIDesignRequiresCompatibility so stock UIKit chrome gets Liquid Glass; raises the Mach-O SDK to 26.0 only if it's older (never lowers it, keeps minos). Needs Xcode `vtool`, macOS only
- `scripts/build.sh`    full pipeline: fetch NeoFreeBird IPA, patch, `make package`, inject with `cyan`
- `.github/workflows/build.yml`  job `ipa`: same pipeline on macOS runners (unsigned IPA, no secrets); job `sign`: ad-hoc signs it (`fastlane/Fastfile`, match certs from gi-os/ios-certs) as `com.gios.liquidbird` for every device registered in the developer account, strips app extensions, uploads `LiquidBird-adhoc` and deletes the unsigned artifact. Never a public release.
- `scripts/install_link.sh [run-id] [minutes]`  on the Mac mini: serves a run's `LiquidBird-adhoc` as a tap-to-install link (Cloudflare quick tunnel, random path, stops after 20 min). Send Giovanni the link; he opens it in Safari on the phone.
- `scripts/register_link.sh [minutes]`  on the Mac mini: one-time link that captures a new device's UDID; then `gh workflow run build.yml -f udid=<udid> -f device_name=<name>` registers it and re-signs.

## Rules
- Class names marked `// placeholder` are unverified. Confirm in FLEX before relying on them: the IPA already ships BHTwitter's FLEX (`libbhFLEX.dylib`, try its toggle in BHTwitter settings first), else inject FLEXing (`scripts/build.sh --flex <FLEXing.deb>`, or run the workflow with `flex`). Placeholder hooks live in their own `%group`, initialised in `%ctor` only if `objc_getClass` finds the class; keep it that way (Logos doesn't check, and a nil class can crash at launch). Prefer matching on accessibilityLabel/identifier over private class names; they survive app updates.
- Guard every iOS 26 API with `if (@available(iOS 26, *))`. Deployment target stays 15.0.
- Following X's code: `scripts/objc_find.py <macho> <selector-regex>` gives a method's address, `scripts/objc_dis.py <macho> <start> <end>` disassembles it with the messages it sends and the classes and strings it loads (how the full-screen viewer's status bar owner, its XNAV wrapper, was found).
- Finding classes: `nm`/strings miss most of them; dump ObjC metadata (class and category method lists) from the frameworks in the NeoFreeBird IPA (T1Twitter, XServiceLibraries, XAppLibraries hold the app). Navigation: T1TabbedAppNavigationViewController (tabs, `currentTabNavigationController`), T1TabBarViewController (`tabViews`, `setTabBarHidden:withDuration:completion:`), TFNCustomTabBar, TFNFloatingActionButton, T1TabNavigationController (`showComposeFromBarButtonItem:`).
- No global hooks on `UIView -layoutSubviews`; too hot. `UIControl` and `UIScrollView -didMoveToWindow` are the widest acceptable hooks.
- Never commit IPAs, decrypted binaries, or signing certs. `.gitignore` covers them.
- The repo is public (free macOS minutes), so the signed IPA (its ad-hoc profile lists the devices) goes up encrypted to the Mac mini: a one-time key encrypted to `artifact-key.pub.pem`, whose private half is only `~/.config/liquidbird/artifact-key.pem` there (`scripts/install_link.sh` decrypts). The unsigned IPA between jobs is NeoFreeBird's public app plus this tweak, and is deleted once signed. Logs must not list devices; a UDID passed as a dispatch input shows in that run.
- Signing secrets (synced from the gi-os/ios-certs hub) go only to the `sign` job, which runs no third-party code; never pass them to `ipa`. Registered so far: Giovanni's iPhone and iPad; the ad-hoc profile lasts until Sep 2027.
